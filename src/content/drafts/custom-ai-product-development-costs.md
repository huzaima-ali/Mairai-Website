# Draft: What custom AI product development costs

Status: unpublished. Do not promote until commercial ranges can be verified from actual Mirai proposals.

## Why this is a draft

Public pricing pages that invent day rates, team sizes or typical project totals would be misleading. Mirai scopes work from the workflow, integrations, oversight needs and delivery model. Those inputs vary too widely for a single published number.

## What a future article should cover

- Cost drivers: discovery, design, model/tool integration, evaluation, security, and ongoing iteration.
- Engagement shapes already described on the site: build with us, scale with us, partner with us.
- What is usually out of scope: training frontier models, unconstrained agents, and speculative Super Intelligence programmes.
- How buyers should compare proposals: job definition, review points, ownership of code and data, and evaluation, not model brand names.

## Next step

Publish only after finance and delivery agree on ranges that match real statements of work.
